After completing an approved activity, Fellows submit expense claims through lowFAT. Claims must be linked to an approved funding request and include a completed University of Edinburgh Non-Staff Expenses Claim Form and all supporting receipts as a single PDF.
Staff are responsible for reviewing claims for completeness and compliance before passing them for payment.

## What to Check
When reviewing an expense claim, staff should verify the following:
- The claim is linked to an approved funding request
- The Fellow has downloaded the latest version of the University of Edinburgh Non-Staff Expenses Claim Form from the SSI website
- Sections 1–5 of the form are fully completed, including:
- Supplier number (if previously issued)
- Full name and home address (not work address)
- Bank details — correct and clearly filled in
- Section 3 'For the purpose of' field — should include the lowFAT ID, Fellow name, and reason (e.g. 777 J Smith - Travel to London for RSECon)
- Name and date in Section 5
- The claim form is saved in MS Word format, named correctly as: LowFAT ID-Initial Family name (e.g. 777 J Smith)
- All receipts are numbered to match Section 4 of the claim form
- Each claimed item has a formal receipt or VAT invoice
- Bank or card statement excerpts are provided where required (foreign currency, accommodation, conference tickets, international flights, part payments from group bills)
- The currency for payment is clearly stated
- All receipts are compiled into a single PDF

## Receipts and Proof of Payment
Staff should ensure Fellows have adhered to the following rules:
- Credit or debit card slips are not sufficient alone — an itemised receipt or invoice must accompany them
- Hotel booking confirmations are not accepted as proof of payment — a VAT invoice after the stay plus a bank statement excerpt is required
- Online order confirmations are not accepted — a VAT invoice is required
- Alcohol must not be included in any claim
- Business or first-class flights or train tickets are not reimbursable
- Internal UK flights are not permitted under the UoE Sustainable Travel Policy
- Equipment such as computers, laptops, tablets, monitors, or printers is not eligible

## Expense Claim Statuses

| Status | Staff Action / Meaning |
|---|---|
| Submitted | Claim received and awaiting staff review. |
| Processing | Claim is being actively reviewed and processed by the Finance team. |
| Returned to claimant for Review/Action | Staff have identified an issue. The Fellow must provide further information, clarification, or supporting documents. |
| Pending for approval | Claim completed and sent to SSI Directorate for internal approval. |
| Approved | Claim approved and sent to University of Edinburgh central Finance for payment. |
| Waiting for blogpost | Claim cannot be processed until the Fellow submits an associated blog post. |
| Rejected | Claim rejected. |
| Cancelled | Cancelled by the reviewer or Fellow. |

📌 Note: All communications with Fellows about their claims (e.g. requests for additional documentation) are logged in lowFAT.

## Amount Discrepancies
If a Fellow's claim amount is more than 20% higher than the originally approved funding request, the Fellow must provide a justification. Staff should review this explanation before approving.

## Invoiced Claims and UoE-Based Fellows
Fellows based at UK universities who are claiming directly from the SSI should not use the standard Non-Staff Expenses Claim Form. Instead, payment is made by Purchase Order and invoice. Staff should:
- Contact SSI Finance to arrange this
- Ensure this process is initiated at least two months before work begins — the University of Edinburgh will not allow work to commence without a Purchase Order in place

## Advance Bookings
In certain cases (e.g. long-haul flights), the SSI can book travel directly on behalf of a Fellow. This incurs administrative overhead and is handled case-by-case. Fellows should email fellows-management@software.ac.uk to discuss this option in advance.

## Payments
Payments to Fellows are typically processed within a few weeks after expense claims are approved. Payment tracking is managed separately from lowFAT by the Finance and Fellowship Programme Team.
