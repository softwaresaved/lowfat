The **Dashboard** is the default page you see when you log in. It provides an overview of your Fellowship activity in one place, including your funding requests, expenses, and blog posts.

<div class="doc-screenshot">
  <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/img/dashboard-2026.png" alt="Example of the lowFAT dashboard" class="img-fluid">
</div>

The page is divided into four sections:

1. **Finances** – a summary of your available, committed, spent, and passed funding  
2. **Funding Requests** – track and manage funding requests you have submitted or that are in progress  
3. **Expenses** – review and track any expenses you’ve claimed  
4. **Blog Posts** – access a list of blog posts you’ve published or are working on  

The tables in the Funding Requests, Expenses, and Blog Posts sections are colour-coded to help you quickly identify the status of items at a glance. 

---

## Finances

This section shows how your Fellowship funding is allocated and helps you understand how much funding remains available.

### Definitions

- **Grant** – the total amount awarded to you as part of your Fellowship  
- **Available** – funding that has not yet been committed or spent  
- **Committed** – funding that has been approved but not yet spent (i.e. not yet claimed through expenses)  
- **Spent** – the total amount of approved expense claims 

During your inaugural year:

> **Available + Committed + Spent = Total Grant**

Once all expense claims for a specific activity have been processed, any difference between the **Committed** and **Spent** amounts will be returned to the **Available** balance.

After your inaugural year:

- **Available** will be set to £0  
- You can still submit funding requests to the **Further Development Fund**

<div class="box-note">
<strong>Please note:</strong><br>

Certain funding requests and expenses (such as in-person events like the Collaborations Workshop) are covered separately by the Institute and are not included in the calculations for Available, Committed, or Spent.
</div>

### Understanding the finance bar

The finance summary uses colour to show how your funds are distributed:

**Green** – Available  
**Orange** – Committed  
**Red** – Spent  
**Black** – Passed  

This provides a quick visual overview of how your funding is currently allocated.

## Funding Requests

This section allows you to track and manage your funding requests.

The table provides an overview of each request, including:

- Unique ID  
- Submission date  
- Title  
- Start and end dates  
- Status  
- Requested amount  
- Approved amount and approval date (if applicable)
- Available actions  

If no funding requests have been submitted, the table will display **“No event.”**

### Statuses

- **Unprocessed** – submitted but not yet reviewed 
- **Processing** – currently under review  
- **Approved** – reviewed and approved to proceed  
- **Archived** – completed and stored for record-keeping purposes 
- **Cancelled** – cancelled by the submitter or reviewer before completion  
- **Rejected** – reviewed and declined. No further action required  
- **Removed** – removed from the workflow

### Important Notes

- For funding requests, **Archived** means that all related expense claims have been submitted and approved.  
  - It does *not* mean that payment has been made.
- Payments are typically processed within a few weeks after expense claims are approved.
- Payments are tracked separately from lowFAT by the Finance and Fellowship Programme Team.

<div class="box-note">
Once your funding request is <strong>approved</strong>, you can no longer make changes within the system. If changes are required, please contact: <a href="mailto:fellows-management@software.ac.uk">fellows-management@software.ac.uk</a>
</div>

## Expenses

This section allows you to track and manage your expense claims.

The table provides:

- Claim details  
- Status  
- Amount claimed  
- Amount authorised for payment  
- Available actions  

You may be contacted by the Finance team regarding your expense claim. These email communications (and your responses) will be logged in lowFAT.

### Statuses

- **Submitted** – awaiting review
- **Returned to claimant for Review/Action** – requires additional clarification, documentation, or receipts
- **Processing** – under review by the Finance team  
- **Pending for approval** – completed and sent to the SSI Directorate for internal approval  
- **Approved** – approved and sent to the University of Edinburgh central finance team for payment  
- **Waiting for Blog Post** – a blog post must be submitted before the claim can be processed 
- **Rejected** – the claim has been rejected.  
- **Cancelled** – the claim has been cancelled by the reviewer or Fellow
- **Removed** – removed from the workflow

---

## Blog Posts

This section allows you to track and manage your blog posts.

The table provides:

- Submission details  
- Status  
- Publish date (if published)  
- Available actions  

### Types of Status

- **Waiting for triage** – awaiting assignment to a reviewer or editor  
- **Waiting to be reviewed** – awaiting review by the assigned reviewer or editor  
- **Reviewing loop** – under review with possible revisions 
- **Waiting to be proofread** – ready for proofreading before publication  
- **Waiting to be published** – ready for publication  
- **Published** – successfully published  
- **Cancelled** – cancelled by the Fellow or reviewer (e.g. content is out of date).
