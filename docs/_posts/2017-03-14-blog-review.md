Fellows are required to submit a blog post following each funded activity. A blog post must be submitted and approved before an associated expense claim can be processed for payment.

Staff are responsible for triaging, assigning reviewers or editors, managing the review loop, and overseeing proofreading and publication.

| Status | Staff Action / Meaning |
|---|---|
| Waiting for triage | Blog post submitted — staff must assign a reviewer or editor. |
| Waiting to be reviewed | Assigned to a reviewer/editor; awaiting their review. |
| Reviewing loop | Blog is under active review; may undergo multiple feedback and revision cycles. |
| Waiting to be proofread | Review complete; blog ready for proofreading. |
| Waiting to be published | Proofreading complete; blog ready for publication. |
| Published | Blog post has been published. |
| Cancelled | Cancelled by the Fellow or reviewer (e.g. the content is out of date). |

## Images in Blog Posts
Fellows are asked not to embed images directly in Google Docs drafts. If a Fellow submits a post with embedded images, staff should ask them to either include a link to the image (if they own it) or send the image separately with a caption. Images can be sent to the Comms Officer or relevant SSI contact.

## Previously Published Posts
If a Fellow has already published a blog post on their personal blog or elsewhere, they should copy the content into a new Google Doc and submit the link. Staff should check this has been done before beginning the review process.
