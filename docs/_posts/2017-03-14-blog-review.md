Fellows are required to submit a blog post following each funded activity.  
A blog post must be submitted and approved before an associated expense claim can be processed for payment.

Staff are responsible for:

- Triaging submissions  
- Assigning reviewers or editors  
- Managing the review loop  
- Overseeing proofreading and publication  

---

## Accessing a Blog Post

Blog posts can be accessed from the **Dashboard**.

- Locate the blog post under the relevant status column  
- Click **“Review”** under the **Actions** column  

This opens the **blog review form**.

---

## Blog Post Statuses

| Status | Staff Action / Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for triage** | Blog post submitted — staff must assign a reviewer or editor |
| **Waiting to be reviewed** | Assigned to a reviewer/editor; awaiting review |
| **Reviewing loop** | Blog is under active review; may go through multiple feedback cycles |
| **Waiting to be proofread** | Review complete; ready for proofreading |
| **Waiting to be published** | Proofreading complete; ready for publication |
| **Published** | Blog post has been published |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled by the Fellow or reviewer (e.g. content is no longer relevant) |

---

## What to Check

When managing a blog post, staff should ensure:

- The blog post is linked to the correct **funding request**  
- The content is relevant to the **funded activity**  
- The draft is accessible (e.g. Google Docs link works)  
- Any reviewer/editor has been assigned where required  

---

## Images in Blog Posts

Fellows are asked **not to embed images directly** in Google Docs drafts.

If a blog post includes embedded images, staff should ask the Fellow to:

- Provide a **link to the image** (if they own it), **or**  
- Send the image separately with a caption  

Images should be sent to the **Comms Officer** or relevant SSI contact.

---

## Previously Published Posts

If a Fellow has already published their blog post elsewhere (e.g. personal blog):

- The content must be **copied into a new Google Doc**  
- The Google Doc link should be submitted via lowFAT  

Staff should confirm this has been done **before starting the review process**.
