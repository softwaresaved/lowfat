## Accessing the Blog Post Form

You can submit a blog post in one of the following ways:

- Go to **“Options”** in the top navigation menu and select **“Submit a blog post”**
- Click the **“Submit a blog post”** button at the top of the dashboard view
- Click the **“Submit a blog post”** button next to the **“Blog Posts”** heading

This will open the blog post submission form.

Follow these steps to complete the submission form.

1. Select the **approved funding request**

   If you do not see your approved funding request listed, please contact:  
   [fellows-management@software.ac.uk](mailto:fellows-management@software.ac.uk)

2. Indicate whether this is the **final version** of your blog post.

   Please note:

   - Your blog post will be reviewed by SSI staff  
   - It may be revised before publication  
   - At this stage, you are simply indicating whether this is your final version  

3. Provide the **names of any co-authors**

   List all co-authors who contributed to the blog post.

4. Provide the **URL of your blog post draft**

   We prefer receiving links to drafts hosted on:

   - Google Docs  
   - Microsoft Office 365  
   - Other online collaborative platforms  

   **Images**

   Please do **not embed images directly** in Google Docs.

   Instead:

   - Include a link to the image if it is your own, **or**  
   - Send the image separately with a caption and reference to the blog post to:  
     - Denis Barclay – [d.barclay@software.ac.uk](mailto:d.barclay@software.ac.uk)  
     - Oscar Seip – [oscar.seip@manchester.ac.uk](mailto:oscar.seip@manchester.ac.uk)  

   If your blog post has already been published (e.g. on your personal blog):

   - Copy the content into a new Google document  
   - Share the link to that document  

5. Describe your **outputs and outcomes**

   Briefly explain what was achieved as a result of your participation or the topic raised.

   **Outputs** (tangible deliverables), such as:

   - Presentation slides  
   - Reports  
   - Software  
   - Datasets  
   - Blog posts  

   **Outcomes** (impact or benefits), such as:

   - New collaborations  
   - Knowledge gained  
   - Skills developed  
   - Changes in practice within your community  

6. Add any additional notes *(optional)*

   Include any relevant comments or clarifications.
